About us

From the groves of Namakkal to ports around the world.

Lucky Exports is a coconut exporter based in Karukkampalayam, Paramathi Velur Taluk, Namakkal District, Tamil Nadu. We source mature coconuts directly from local growers and prepare them for long sea voyages.

Every shipment is handled end to end — sorting, packing, loading and transport to the port — so importers receive a single, clear FOB price and a reliable delivery window.

  • Direct sourcing from Tamil Nadu coconut farms
  • Careful grading, packing and container loading
  • Transparent proforma invoicing and payment milestones

How it works

Farm to freight in five steps.

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Mature coconuts collected from partner farms around Namakkal.

  2. 02

    Husking & grading

    Semi-husked, sorted by size and weight, damaged nuts removed.

  3. 03

    Packing

    Packed for ventilation and loaded carefully into containers.

  4. 04

    Port of Tuticorin

    Road transport, customs clearance and loading on board.

  5. 05

    At sea & delivered

    Shipped to your port within 45–60 days of order.

About us

From the groves of Namakkal to ports around the world.

Lucky Exports is a coconut exporter based in Karukkampalayam, Paramathi Velur Taluk, Namakkal District, Tamil Nadu. We source mature coconuts directly from local growers and prepare them for long sea voyages.

Every shipment is handled end to end — sorting, packing, loading and transport to the port — so importers receive a single, clear FOB price and a reliable delivery window.

Shipping terms (Incoterms®)

FOB Tuticorin our standard

We pack, load and clear the cargo for export and put it on board the vessel. You book the ocean freight and insurance. Risk passes to you once the goods are on board.

CFR your port

We also book and pay the ocean freight to your port. You insure the cargo and clear it on arrival.

CIF your port

As CFR, plus we arrange the minimum marine insurance cover. Import clearance stays with you.

What does FOB mean, and who pays for freight?

FOB (free on board) means we deliver the goods cleared for export and loaded on the vessel at Tuticorin. From that point you arrange and pay the ocean freight and insurance. If you would rather not manage shipping, ask for a CFR or CIF quote and we book the freight to your port.

How long does an order take?

About 45–60 days from confirmed order to delivery. Preparing and clearing the cargo takes around two to three weeks, and the sea voyage makes up most of the rest, depending on your route.

How do payments work?

50% advance when you confirm the proforma invoice, 20% within 10 days, and the final 30% before dispatch. All payments are in US dollars by bank transfer.

Who handles customs and paperwork?

We handle export customs, the phytosanitary certificate and the shipping documents in India. At your end, your customs broker clears the cargo and pays import duty and taxes.

Can I track my container?

Yes. After loading you receive the bill of lading, container number and vessel name, which you can use to follow the cargo on the shipping line's website.

What if the goods arrive damaged or short?

Photograph the cargo, note the condition at delivery and tell us within the agreed days. Transit damage is claimed through the marine insurance, and quality or quantity differences are settled against the packing list and inspection report.

Can I send an inspector or request a sample?

Yes. Ask for a sample before ordering, or appoint an independent inspector to check the goods before the container is sealed.
